# 网络购物：方便还是麻烦？

## Lesson brief

- **Audience:** one adult or university HSK4 learner working one-to-one with a teacher; the learner can discuss familiar experiences in short connected sentences.
- **Duration:** 45–60 minutes.
- **Communication job:** By the end, the learner can use HSK4-level language to compare online-shopping benefits and problems, ask follow-up questions about a purchase, and give a supported personal view.
- **Teaching outcomes:** the learner can (1) understand and use the target vocabulary and patterns; (2) describe an online-shopping experience with a problem and response; (3) compare two choices using 因为…所以… / 虽然…但是…; and (4) complete a short buyer–seller or buyer–friend role-play with the teacher.
- **Pedagogical arc:** activate experience → notice language → understand a short scenario → practise controlled choices → negotiate a problem → state and support a judgement → retrieve and extend.

## Shared lesson system

- **Terminology:** 网络购物、商品、下单、付款、快递、卖家、买家、评价、退货、方便、麻烦、质量、折扣、合适、推荐； patterns: 一…就…、因为…所以…、虽然…但是…、跟…一样 / 不一样、要是…就… .
- **Visual direction:** calm e-commerce journey: warm orange for actions, blue for questions/problems, simple parcel/phone/product imagery; large Chinese, pinyin only when first introducing new words; no dense grids.
- **Source and claim policy:** all examples, prices, messages, and products are invented classroom material. Make no factual claims about platforms, delivery times, consumer law, or market trends. Any later external asset or non-trivial claim needs an attributable source in speaker notes.
- **Assessment evidence:** teacher listens for accurate target vocabulary, one comparison, one cause/result or concession pattern, and a comprehensible response to a shopping problem.

## Slide storyboard

### 1. 网络购物：方便还是麻烦？

- **Purpose/content:** open the question with a phone and parcel visual; show the title and two contrasting icons.
- **Activity:** learner chooses and says “更方便 / 更麻烦 / 看情况”; teacher asks for one reason.
- **Acceptance:** title is immediately readable; no answer is implied; learner gives or receives a scaffold for one reason.

### 2. 你上次买了什么？

- **Purpose/content:** activate experience with product pictures: 书、衣服、耳机、零食.
- **Activity:** teacher interviews the learner: “你上次在网上买了什么？” “为什么？”.
- **Visual direction:** four clear product images, not platform branding.
- **Acceptance:** learner produces or points to one product and answers the teacher's follow-up question.

### 3. 购物的一条路

- **Purpose/content:** introduce sequence vocabulary: 看商品 → 下单 → 付款 → 等快递 → 收包裹 → 评价.
- **Activity:** learner puts mixed action cards in order, reads them aloud, and teacher prompts a correction if needed.
- **Visual direction:** left-to-right journey with one phone-to-parcel line.
- **Acceptance:** learner sequences all six actions and pronounces the target words intelligibly.

### 4. 方便在哪里？

- **Purpose/content:** model reasons: “不用出门”“可以比较价格”“商品很多”. Introduce 因为…所以… .
- **Activity:** teacher gives two picture cues; learner completes two oral cause/result prompts.
- **Visual direction:** home, comparison, choice imagery; orange action accents.
- **Acceptance:** learner makes one complete 因为…所以… sentence without reading the whole answer.

### 5. 麻烦在哪里？

- **Purpose/content:** present problems: 快递慢、质量不一样、衣服不合适、退货麻烦. Contrast 虽然…但是… .
- **Activity:** learner chooses a problem card and says “虽然…，但是…”, with the teacher asking one follow-up.
- **Visual direction:** one damaged-looking generic box, a waiting clock, a size mismatch.
- **Acceptance:** learner distinguishes problem vocabulary from slide 4 benefits and forms one contrast.

### 6. 小李的耳机怎么了？

- **Purpose/content:** short illustrated scenario: 小李下单耳机；收到后声音有问题；他联系卖家并申请退货.
- **Activity:** teacher reads the scenario aloud; learner listens/reads and answers: 买了什么？有什么问题？他怎么办？
- **Visual direction:** three-panel story with a message bubble; speaker notes hold the teacher read-aloud text.
- **Acceptance:** learner answers all three questions using key vocabulary, with teacher prompts allowed.

### 7. 这两件衣服，选哪一件？

- **Purpose/content:** two invented product listings differing in colour, price, and size; practise 跟…一样 / 不一样 and 比较.
- **Activity:** learner chooses one listing and explains two reasons to the teacher.
- **Visual direction:** two large unbranded clothing cards with sparse, legible labels.
- **Acceptance:** learner says one similarity/difference and one preference reason.

### 8. 客服对话：我想退货

- **Purpose/content:** language bank for a polite exchange: “我收到…了，但是…” “可以退货吗？” “请把…发给我.”
- **Activity:** teacher models once; learner arranges four dialogue turns, then speaks the buyer while the teacher speaks the seller.
- **Visual direction:** buyer/seller speech bubbles, clear turn labels.
- **Acceptance:** learner reconstructs a logical four-turn exchange and identifies the request and response.

### 9. 角色扮演：买家、卖家、观察员

- **Purpose/content:** task card: wrong colour / broken item / size too small; response choices: 换货、退货、再等一天.
- **Activity:** teacher switches roles: seller while the learner is buyer; buyer while the learner is seller; then observer while the learner replays both turns. The teacher uses the checklist for feedback.
- **Visual direction:** scenario cards with a minimal checklist.
- **Acceptance:** learner explains a problem, makes a request, and gives a relevant reply in the role switch; teacher-observer records one successful pattern and one next step.

### 10. 方便还是麻烦？看情况

- **Purpose/content:** synthesis frame: “网络购物很方便，因为…；但是…也会很麻烦.”
- **Activity:** learner places one choice marker on a continuum and gives one supported view; teacher challenges it with “为什么？”.
- **Visual direction:** balanced scale, not a winner/loser comparison.
- **Acceptance:** learner gives a supported opinion using at least one target connector.

### 11. 给朋友的购物建议

- **Purpose/content:** transfer task: a friend wants to buy a birthday gift online tonight. Prompt: what should they check before 下单？
- **Activity:** learner creates and tells the teacher, acting as the friend, a three-sentence recommendation using 要是…就… .
- **Visual direction:** gift and checklist imagery, three open writing lines.
- **Acceptance:** learner's recommendation includes a condition, an action, and a reason; no platform-specific advice is required.

### 12. Exit ticket：我会怎么说？

- **Purpose/content:** retrieve three prompts: one new word, one shopping problem, one personal conclusion.
- **Activity:** learner gives a 30-second spoken or written response; teacher records evidence for follow-up.
- **Visual direction:** quiet recap with ample writing space.
- **Acceptance:** response contains one accurate term and one complete linked sentence; teacher can identify the learner's need for localized rework next lesson.

## Notes for production

Slides 3–5 may be a visual-language batch; slides 6–9 are a scenario-to-role-play batch. Each slide remains independently replaceable, and speaker notes should contain only teacher cues, model read-aloud text, and required source attribution.
