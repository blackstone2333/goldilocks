## Interpretation

1. Goldilocks achieved the intended quality parity with Superpowers in this round.
2. Its dynamic compression worked on non-trivial debugging and alignment work.
3. Its Direct path is not lean enough yet: the completion entry turned a one-character edit into five tool calls and 107k tokens.
4. Baseline and Ponytail were cheaper on this small suite. A capable Terra model already solved all three prompts, and the Papa prompt itself specified the expected decision behavior.
5. Cache distribution varied substantially between cells. Total, cached, and uncached input must remain separate, and `n=1` cannot support a public speed claim.

## Next evidence-driven change

Test a v0.2.2 trigger refinement that prevents `verification-before-completion` from implicitly loading on clear Direct work while preserving one targeted check. Rerun Baby at least three times before accepting the change; then repeat Mama and Papa to confirm the quality floor did not move.
