# Brainstorming

Read [align.md](../goldilocks/references/align.md) and follow it. Inspect project facts first, identify only decisions that can change the outcome, recommend a direction with trade-offs, and define acceptance. Do not force a design ritual for already-clear low-risk work. Do not implement while a material decision remains silently assumed. Return to the smallest applicable Goldilocks route after alignment.
